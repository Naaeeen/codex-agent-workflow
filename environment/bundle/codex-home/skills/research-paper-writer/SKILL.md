# Research paper workflow

Use for full academic papers and substantial paper revision. A short course
comment, explanation or study exercise does not require a full paper workflow.
Preserve the requested voice, word limit and disclosure requirements.

1. Establish the contribution, available evidence, audience, article type and
   requested venue from the supplied material. Ask only about unresolved facts
   that prevent progress; otherwise label reasonable draft assumptions.
2. Make a concise outline appropriate to that article. A survey needs its actual
   search/selection method; an experimental paper needs real methods/results;
   a proposal labels its planned work. Do not invent studies to fill a template.
3. Maintain a claim-to-source/result map. Verify reference identity and what
   each source actually supports. Distinguish reported findings, your synthesis,
   limitations and hypotheses; no fabricated results, citations or novelty.
4. Draft sections in a useful order. Keep technical terms/notation consistent,
   make comparisons specific, and ensure abstract/conclusion match the evidence.
   First/third person and structural conventions follow the venue and user.
5. Use the selected venue's current official author instructions and template.
   When unspecified, deliver a venue-neutral draft rather than guessing IEEE/ACM.
6. Check required content, references, evidence, word/page limit and requested
   disclosure. Use the host document/PDF/LaTeX tools for layout and real rendering.
   A successful compilation does not establish scientific or submission readiness.

Read only relevant optional references:

- references/writing_style_guide.md: examples of academic structure/style;
  current venue/user requirements take precedence.
- references/ieee_formatting_specs.md or references/acm_formatting_specs.md,
  and the bundled example PDFs: historical layout examples, not current rules.

This is an instruction workflow. The old JavaScript packaging stub that returned
a success string without implementing anything has been removed from the active
skill. It has no programmatic paper-generation API.
