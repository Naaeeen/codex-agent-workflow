# Prepare a short, evidence-based course plan

Use the course profile and information already supplied. Collect only what changes the plan: actual exam date or remaining-day budget, daily time, course scope and starting knowledge. A range such as 3–7 days is not an exact exam date. Ask concise missing questions once; useful source inspection may proceed while waiting.

Use official learning objectives, teacher instructions, original slides and assessed exercises as scope evidence. Keep inferred emphasis and unknown coverage visible. Do not convert the frequency of a topic in a few questions into an exact exam probability.

Default course roots are materials/slides, materials/problems and materials/solutions; materials/holdout is reserved. Respect a different configured or user-named location. Research reports, installed skills and generated study records are not course content.

Check representative originals for page order, symbols, equations and diagrams. Build study/source-index.md incrementally: source file, verified page/slide locations, topic IDs, original question identities and matched answer locations. For repeated question numbers preserve section identity and inspect both statements.

Keep study/coverage.md as a concise table of topic ID, source scope, prerequisite, assessed task, priority basis, observed learning state and next check. Include slide sections not yet matched to a problem; lack of a practice question does not prove they are outside the exam.

Plan in short learning blocks:
- About 3 days: prioritize explicit assessed core concepts and essential prerequisites, teach then practice; keep time for unresolved errors and an independent final check.
- About 4–7 days: combine new learning with next-day retrieval and mixed questions; adjust from observed work.
- Any period: use the actual daily budget when known. State what is deferred and why, without promising a score.

Start teaching after a compact initial map. Do not require complete knowledge lists, spreadsheets, PDFs, a full diagnostic exam or every topic's materials to be processed before the first useful lesson.

This adapts OpenAI's [Build an exam study system](https://learn.chatgpt.com/use-cases/build-exam-study-system): align practice with official course sources, keep unclear coverage as a question, and revise future sessions around actual gaps. The published example uses two weeks; the short timeline here is a user-requested adaptation.
