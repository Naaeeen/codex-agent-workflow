# Teach, practice, repair and revisit

Use the selected flow in the course profile. Match the workload to the available session and remaining days; never assume zero knowledge or expertise without evidence.

## Guided practice (selected default)

Teach a small coherent concept, including any prerequisite or notation the learner needs. Explain what the definition or formula means, its conditions and when it fails. Work one representative example with a reason for each important step and a result check.

Then ask the learner to complete a key step or attempt a corresponding original problem. Wait for the real answer. Next, when useful, change a condition and ask for an independent application; later mix with earlier topics so choosing a method is part of the task.

Compress familiar prerequisites. A learner who is missing the notation needs an explanation, not repeated Socratic questions. When explicitly stuck or requesting a solution, give a direct worked explanation and arrange an unseen retry.

## Other selectable flows

Problem-first: start with the original problem; use the learner's attempt to find the gap, teach it with an example, then retry. Do not keep quizzing on an unlearned prerequisite.

Slides-first: follow one bounded slide section, explain its conceptual links and inspect important original figures; practice after the section. Mark material not yet covered.

Daily-pack: provide a complete block the learner can use at their own pace. Include the necessary concepts, examples, exercises, time allocation and answers after the independent exercises. Generate Markdown/chat by default; produce a PDF only when requested.

A flow switch preserves the same source index, topic IDs, actual attempts and errors.

## Feedback and evidence

Identify the exact point in the learner's work that is right or wrong. Compare with the correctly paired official answer, allowing equivalent sound methods. Distinguish concept, method, arithmetic, interpretation and time-pressure errors only when supported by the answer. Mark suspected causes as unconfirmed.

Record the original answer before giving the correction. Tag assistance: none, hint or solution. Seeing or paraphrasing a provided solution is not independent performance.

A generated variation must be labelled as such. Independently solve/check it before using it, and keep generated answer confidence separate from an official answer key. When no official key exists, record a provisional model assessment.

Do not auto-score numerical equivalence with a generic tolerance. Inspect fractions, percentages, signs, units, dimensions and the requested precision. Do not turn a single success into a mastery claim.

Use the helper to preserve actual attempts and review dates. Update coverage and future work from this evidence. A hint-assisted success may show progress but still needs an independent check. Revisit unresolved errors next day when possible; use delayed or mixed questions before treating learning as stable.

Retain a small untouched official mock and its answer key for the final independent check. Do not index or reveal the key in ordinary learning.

## Session close

Save the actual checkpoint, identify the one next task and list the important uncovered material. Do not invent completed work or fabricate a wrong answer to populate a log.

The teaching sequence is a design adaptation of good-learning. Research on worked examples, fading and retrieval motivates the approach; it does not prove a fixed number of questions or this skill is optimal for every learner.
