# Evidence summary

BENCH-NEW records throughput 100 to 120, a 25% improvement on one synthetic workload.
Its p95 latency rises from 100 to 140 ms, a 40% increase, so lower latency is contradicted.
DESIGN-OLD's twofold speedup was a target; the observed result does not support that claim.
LIMITS records one observation and no cross-workload evaluation. We cannot establish
that all workloads are faster or that human users benefit. Further repeated trials are needed.
